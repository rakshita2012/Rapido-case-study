01 The problem

The cheapest way to work takes a lot of thought.

A Rapido bike taxi costs ₹8–10 per km against ₹22–25 for a cab, and women take about 35% of Rapido’s rides in larger cities. I take one twice a day. As a woman, choosing it means weighing comfort and safety every time.

Rapido’s trust checks end at the OTP. It proves who holds the phone, not who is riding, which bike arrived, or how it is ridden.

How might we make every bike ride verifiable and safe from pickup to drop?

KPMG, Unlocking the Potential of Bike-Taxis in India (2024); Aravind Sanka, YourStory (Nov 2025)

Stop 2 During the ride

80–86%

of my rides had irresponsible riding: 40–43 of 50. Scrolling reels on the mounted phone, speeding, riding on the wrong side to save a few kilometres.

  • 46.2% of India’s 1,77,175 road deaths in 2024 were two-wheeler riders and pillions.
  • Overspeeding is linked to about 70% of road deaths.
  • No platform in India publicly reports speed alerts, riding scores or phone-use detection.

04 Goals

North star: the share of rides completed verified, safe and on time.

  1. Verify every ride at pickupMismatch reports per 1,000 rides
  2. Make riding visibleSpeeding and wrong-side events per 100 km
  3. Keep accepted ridesCaptain cancellations after acceptance
  4. Add supply at office peaksShare of peak requests fulfilled
  5. Add value beyond the rideVerified Hopr Connect profiles; repeat rides

Baselines come from a pilot; Rapido does not publish these numbers today

Fix 1 Live pickup check

Face and plate, proved together, live.

  1. At pickup, the captain takes one live photo showing his face and the number plate. No gallery uploads.
  2. A blink-or-turn liveness check, so a printed photo fails.
  3. The plate is matched to the VAHAN registry: number, make and model.
  4. The rider sees “Verified at pickup” before sharing the OTP. “Doesn’t match” pauses the ride.

Checks run at random pickups, so they can’t be staged. Repeat mismatches lead to suspension.

Fix 2 Captain profile

Reviews riders can trust, including from women.

  1. Before pickup, the rider opens the captain’s profile: verified photo, registered bike, rating.
  2. Written reviews show their count and recency, so 4 reviews never look like 400.
  3. A “Reviews from women riders” tab shows only what women wrote.

Reviews publish late and in batches, never linked to a ride, so a captain can’t tell who wrote one or where she was picked up.

Fix 3 Safe-ride mode

Riding behaviour, visible and counted.

  1. Navigation inside the Rapido app, so captains never need to leave it.
  2. GPS flags speeding and wrong-side riding; motion sensors flag harsh braking.
  3. If Rapido leaves the screen while the bike moves, the captain is alerted and the seconds are logged.
  4. The rider can tap “Unsafe riding” mid-ride. Events feed a riding score tied to incentives.

No phone lock: an app can’t lock a personal phone, and multi-app captains would leave.

Fix 4 Commute mode

Part-time captains’ commutes become peak supply.

  1. The captain sets home, office, departure window and maximum detour.
  2. In that window he sees only requests along his route, at the normal fare or a small optional discount.
  3. Framed as “share your commute”, with a Commuter captain badge.

A late or dropped commute ride counts against the captain, and the rider is re-matched at once.

Fix 5 Hopr Connect

New in the city? Make the commute a learning ride.

A fresher moving to a metro arrives with theory, few friends and no alumni network to lean on. Long workdays leave little time to meet people or learn from them.

  1. Rider and driver both verify their ID and work email before Connect unlocks.
  2. The rider picks an opt-in learning ride: explore a new domain, or go deeper in her own.
  3. She’s matched with a verified professional on her route. Pickup and drop are at public points, so no home address is shared.
  4. Either person can report the other or leave the match at any time.

A quiet ride stays the default, women can match with women only, and no job or referral is promised. It lives on Hopr because private cars can’t carry paying passengers without a commercial permit. Today Hopr is Bengaluru only, with about 6,500 active users.
